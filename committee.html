Sponsors

We are actively seeking sponsorship support for PRISM 2028. The symposium will bring together leading researchers, senior academicians, industry practitioners, IIT Bombay students, and alumni working across image processing, computer vision, machine learning, perception, signal processing, and intelligent systems.

Sponsoring PRISM 2028 offers a unique opportunity to engage with a focused and highly relevant technical audience. Sponsors will have avenues to interact with participants for research collaboration, talent engagement, hiring, technology discussions, and professional networking.

Organizations interested in supporting the symposium are encouraged to contact the organizing team for sponsorship details.
